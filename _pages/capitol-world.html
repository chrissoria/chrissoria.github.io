A pixel-art map of American government. It opens on a map of the United States: pick a state to see its counties, cities and congressional districts, and step from there into Congress, the White House, the Supreme Court, the California Legislature, or the chambers of San Diego's county board and city council. In Congress and in Sacramento each sheet of paper is a bill, stacked at the committee, calendar or floor its latest action left it at. The data refreshes every morning from public sources.
