A pixel-art map of where the active bills of the U.S. Congress are right now. Legislators sit in their chambers, and each sheet of paper is a bill, stacked at the committee, calendar or floor its latest action left it at. On load, the map replays the latest day of activity. The data refreshes every morning from Congress.gov.
